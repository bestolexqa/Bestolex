 Road Blocker 
 SAB TECH’s Hydraulic Road blockers offer robust and effective security protection for sensitive areas, ensuring unparalleled defense against unwanted intruder attacks.\nOur road blockers provide seamless traffic flow for authorized vehicles while deterring unauthorized vehicular intrusion.\nPowered by a hydraulic power unit (HPU), our road blockers can rapidly lift and lower (in 4-6 seconds), ensuring swift operation.\nIn the event of a power failure, a specially fitted hand pump allows for manual operation, ensuring continuous security even under adverse conditions.

 Frequently Asked Questions 
 What is the difference between a road blocker and a tyre killer? + 
 A road blocker is a steel barrier that rises to close the lane, while a tyre killer uses steel spikes to disable the tyres of a vehicle crossing in the forbidden direction. The choice depends on the threat level and the site, and is confirmed after a site survey. What are the dimensions and operating speed? + 
 Raised height 800–1,100 mm, blocking width 2,000–6,000 mm, raise or lower time 4–6 seconds and a 4.0–7.5 kW hydraulic power pack. What happens during a power failure? + 
 It is operated manually with a fitted hydraulic hand pump, so control of the entrance is maintained. Is the road blocker crash-rated? + 
 This standard model has no crash-test certificate. If the project requires an anti-ram rating, choose a tested model: the RB3M1000CT high-security road blocker (PAS 68 and IWA 14-1) or the RBS3MCT high-security shallow road blocker (PAS 68), both tested in Germany with the truck not advancing beyond the barrier. Does Bestolex supply, install and maintain it in Qatar? + 
 Yes. Bestolex handles the full project in Qatar: site survey and requirements study, supply, installation or technical supervision, testing and commissioning, operator training, then preventive and corrective maintenance and spare parts. The product carries a 1-year warranty.
